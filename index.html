WHAT IS NUWA?

Tobacco-free. Smoke-free. Simply NUWA.

NUWA is a tobacco-free nicotine pouch designed for adult nicotine users who want a cleaner, more discreet alternative to smoking and vaping. Place a pouch under your upper lip, and enjoy smooth flavor and steady strength with no smoke, no vapor, and no mess.

Every NUWA pouch is crafted for comfort and consistency, with carefully selected ingredients, balanced flavors, and a slim design that fits seamlessly into your day.

  • Tobacco-freeMade without tobacco leaf.
  • DiscreetSlim pouches that fit anywhere, anytime.
  • ConsistentReliable flavor and strength in every pouch.

NUWA is intended for adult nicotine users only. Not for non-users.
